# S4 companion — IRS 2027 retirement contribution limits

- Source: Internal Revenue Service, IR-2026-136
- Source URL: https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/401k-limit-increases-to-25500-for-2027-ira-limit-remains-7500
- Release date: November 12, 2026
- Accessed: November 16, 2026
- Excerpt type: selected 2027 limits, paraphrased from the IRS release.

The IRS announced that the 2027 employee elective-deferral limit for 401(k), 403(b), most governmental 457 plans, and the federal Thrift Savings Plan is $25,500, up from $24,500 in 2026. The general catch-up limit for employees age 50 and over in most of those plans is $8,500. A higher catch-up limit of $11,750 applies to participants who attain age 60, 61, 62, or 63 in 2027. The annual IRA contribution limit remains $7,500, and the IRA catch-up limit for people age 50 and over remains $1,100.
