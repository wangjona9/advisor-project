# HarborView Wealth — Model Portfolio Research Note

The classroom model portfolio contains 60% diversified global equities and 40% investment-grade bonds. In the fictional research scenario, its purpose is to illustrate how asset classes with different risk drivers can be combined. It is not a recommendation for any particular client.

Historical returns are sensitive to the measurement period. A strategy that ranked first over one five-year period may perform poorly in a later period. Research comparisons should include risk, drawdowns, fees, and the economic conditions represented in the sample.

The research team does not use a single historical return ranking as sufficient evidence for recommending a portfolio.
