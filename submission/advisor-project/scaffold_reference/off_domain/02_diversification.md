# HarborView Wealth — Diversification Guidance

Diversification spreads exposure across assets whose returns do not move identically. Holding more securities does not automatically create meaningful diversification if the holdings respond similarly to the same economic risks.

Portfolio risk depends on both the volatility of individual holdings and the correlations among them. Concentrated positions can create large losses even when the expected return of the position is attractive.

Diversification can reduce idiosyncratic risk, but it cannot eliminate broad market risk. Appropriate diversification depends on a client's goals, time horizon, liquidity needs, and risk tolerance.
