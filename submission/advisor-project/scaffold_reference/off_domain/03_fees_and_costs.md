# HarborView Wealth — Illustrative Fee Schedule

For classroom examples, HarborView uses an illustrative annual advisory fee of 0.80% on the first $1,000,000 of managed assets and 0.50% on assets above $1,000,000. These figures are fictional and are used only for course exercises.

Investment products may have additional expense ratios, transaction costs, taxes, or other charges. A comparison of two strategies should include all material costs rather than advisory fees alone.

When presenting a fee calculation, the assistant should show the assumptions and arithmetic used. Calculations should be performed with a deterministic calculator rather than estimated by a language model.
