# S4 — IRS, 401(k) limit increases to $24,500 for 2026

- Source: Internal Revenue Service, IR-2025-111; technical detail in Notice 2025-67
- Source URL: https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/401k-limit-increases-to-24500-for-2026-ira-limit-increases-to-7500
- Release date: November 13, 2025
- Accessed: November 16, 2026
- Excerpt type: selected contribution limits, paraphrased from the announcement.

For tax year 2026, the employee elective-deferral limit for 401(k), 403(b), most governmental 457 plans, and the federal Thrift Savings Plan is $24,500. The general age-50-and-over catch-up limit for most of those plans is $8,000. The IRA annual contribution limit is $7,500, and the IRA catch-up limit for people age 50 and over is $1,100.

## Currency note

These are 2026 figures. The IRS announced 2027 limits on November 12, 2026; see the dated 2027 companion excerpt in `corpus/13_irs_limits_2027.md`. Do not present the 2026 limit as current for 2027.
