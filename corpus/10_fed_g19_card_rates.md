# S3 — Federal Reserve, Consumer Credit (G.19), credit card terms

- Source: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, G.19 Consumer Credit release
- Source URL: https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/current/
- Release date: November 6, 2026 (September 2026 release; includes third-quarter 2026 data)
- Accessed: November 16, 2026
- Excerpt type: credit card annual percentage rate (APR) rows from the terms-of-credit table.

For the third quarter of 2026, the Federal Reserve reported an average APR of 21.19% for all credit card accounts and 22.36% for accounts assessed interest. These are national averages, not an individual cardholder's rate. The G.19 release defines the all-accounts rate as the stated APR averaged across credit card accounts at reporting banks. The rate for accounts assessed interest is calculated from finance charges and the balances on which they were assessed.

## Grounding boundary

Use these as dated national reference values when comparing with a user's stated APR. Do not characterize either average as the user's own rate or as a prescribed threshold for “high-interest” debt. The rates update with later releases.
