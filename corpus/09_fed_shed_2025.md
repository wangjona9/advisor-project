# S2 — Federal Reserve, Economic Well-Being of U.S. Households in 2025

- Source: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, 2025 Survey of Household Economics and Decisionmaking (SHED)
- Source URL: https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2026-economic-well-being-of-us-households-in-2025-savings-investments.htm
- Report released: May 13, 2026
- Accessed: November 16, 2026
- Excerpt type: selected 2025 emergency-expense results, paraphrased from the Savings and Investments chapter, Figures 24–25 and accompanying text.

Among adults surveyed, 63% said they would cover a hypothetical $400 emergency expense completely using cash or its equivalent. This was unchanged from 2022–2024. Asked how they would otherwise cover such an expense, 15% said they would use a credit card and pay it over time, 10% would borrow from friends or family, 7% would sell something, 3% would use a bank loan or line of credit, 2% would use a payday loan, deposit advance, or overdraft, and 12% said they could not pay the expense at that time. Respondents could select multiple alternatives.

The report cautions that some people who would not pay entirely with cash may still have access to cash but choose to preserve it as a buffer. The results describe the surveyed adult population; they do not estimate an individual user's probability of handling an emergency.

## Grounding boundary

These are population-level survey results about a hypothetical $400 expense. They do not specify an appropriate personal reserve target or a priority order for allocating surplus.
